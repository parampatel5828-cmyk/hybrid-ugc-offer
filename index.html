The Hybrid Workflow

6 Steps: Intelligence → Concepts → Curation → Production → Scale → Compound

AI handles volume and variations. Humans handle trust and authenticity. Data compounds every cycle.

1

Intelligence Brief

Client submits product URL + goals (2 min). AI scans competitor ads, winning hooks, format trends (30 sec). Strategist adds brand voice. Output: data-backed brief with 3–5 angle recommendations.

AI Scan Competitor Intel Strategist Review
2

AI Concept Generation

LLM generates 20–100 script concepts (hooks, structures, CTAs). AI scores each on hook strength, retention prediction, brand fit. Top 20% surfaced for human review. Output: ranked concept list with predicted scores.

Groq/Gemini Predictive Scoring Volume Generation
3

Human Curation + Matching

Creative director selects top 3–10 concepts. Algorithm matches creators by niche, past performance, style. Creators receive script, shot list, brand guidelines, product. Output: creator assignments with filming instructions.

Creative Director Creator Matching Brief Distribution
4

Human Production

Creators film in 48h (authentic environments, real product use). In-app coaching: lighting, energy, hook delivery (AI-assisted). Raw footage uploaded → auto-transcribed, tagged. Output: raw footage library owned by client.

48h Turnaround Authentic Filming Auto-Tagging
5

AI Post-Production

AI edits: rough cuts, captions, music, branding (minutes). Generates 5–20 variations per winner: hook swaps, CTA variations, aspect ratios, language dubs, length cuts. Auto-QC: compliance, specs, audio. Output: 50–200 ready-to-test variants.

CapCut/HeyGen ElevenLabs Dubs Auto-QC
6

Performance Loop

Client connects ad accounts (Meta, TikTok, YouTube). Auto-track: spend, CTR, hook rate, hold rate, CPA, ROAS. Creative analytics: frame-by-frame retention, winning elements. Weekly AI insights feed back to Step 1. Output: compound creative intelligence.

Meta/TikTok API Frame Analytics Auto-Insights
